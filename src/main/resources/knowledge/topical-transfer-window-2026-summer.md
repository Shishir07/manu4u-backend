TOPIC: Manchester United summer 2026 transfer window — Carrick's first rebuild
CATEGORY: topical   AS OF: 2026-07-20   STATUS: volatile (window open until early September 2026 — update on every significant move)

The summer 2026 transfer window is Michael Carrick's first as permanent Manchester United head coach, and his first chance to heavily shape the squad following his permanent appointment on 22 May 2026. The window's business has centred on a midfield rebuild, made urgent by Casemiro's departure and a long-term injury to Manuel Ugarte, with a third midfielder and a left-back still being pursued as of 20 July 2026.

Confirmed incomings as of 20 July 2026:
- Andrey Santos, midfielder, signed from Chelsea — Michael Carrick's first signing as permanent head coach. Fee: £48 million plus £2 million in add-ons, with a 10% sell-on clause for Chelsea. Santos, 22, moved to Chelsea from Vasco da Gama in 2023 and spent loan spells at Nottingham Forest and Strasbourg (voted into the 2024-25 Ligue 1 team of the season); he has 6 caps for Brazil but was not part of Carlo Ancelotti's 2026 World Cup squad. Analysis (Opta) rates him a stronger passer under pressure than Casemiro, capable of playing a holding or attacking role alongside Kobbie Mainoo.
- Youri Tielemans, midfielder, signed from Aston Villa via a reported £35 million release clause. Tielemans, 29, captains Belgium (90 caps, captained them at the 2026 World Cup, where they lost 2-1 to Spain in the quarter-final) and has 244 Premier League appearances, joining Villa from Leicester (who signed him from Monaco in 2019) on a free transfer. Combined, the Santos and Tielemans deals total roughly £85 million.
- Karl Darlow, goalkeeper, signed from Leeds United on a free transfer.
- Tynan Thompson, 18-year-old winger, signed from Tottenham Hotspur for an initial £4 million plus up to £4 million in add-ons (total package up to £8 million), with a 15% sell-on clause retained by Spurs. Thompson joined Tottenham's academy at 12, scored 13 goals with 6 assists across Under-18s to Under-21s level in 2025-26 (including 7 goals in 7 UEFA Youth League games), and had broken into Spurs' Champions League matchday squads. He joins Manchester United's Under-21s with a pathway to the first team.

A deal that did NOT go through: Manchester United had agreed terms to sign Éderson (a defensive midfielder, not the Manchester City goalkeeper of the same name) from Atalanta for an initial fee reported as £35 million (€40.5 million), intended as a second midfield addition alongside Santos. The move was delayed by Éderson's late World Cup call-up for Brazil and was ultimately called off after the player failed his medical; Manchester United "pulled the plug" on the transfer. Carrick moved on to sign Youri Tielemans instead.

Confirmed outgoings as of 20 July 2026:
- Casemiro left Manchester United at the end of his contract (released), ending the Brazilian midfielder's spell at the club that began in 2022.
- Rasmus Højlund was transferred permanently to Napoli.
- André Onana was loaned to Trabzonspor for a second consecutive season.
- Tyrell Malacia left the club at the end of his contract, leaving Manchester United short of back-up at left-back.

Current targets (interest reported, not signed, as of 20 July 2026): Michael Carrick wants a third midfielder of "established pedigree" — Bournemouth's Alex Scott and Crystal Palace's Adam Wharton have been linked, each valued at a minimum of £80 million. At left-back, Manchester United are monitoring Newcastle's Lewis Hall (under contract to 2029, on loan from Chelsea before a permanent £28m-plus-£7m-add-ons move; Newcastle reluctant to sell after already losing Sandro Tonali and Anthony Gordon this summer) and Fulham's Antonee Robinson (29 next month, contract to 2028) — Manchester United's preference otherwise skews younger than Robinson's profile.

Injury context driving the rebuild: Manuel Ugarte suffered long-term knee ligament damage playing for Uruguay in their 2026 World Cup defeat by Spain, compounding the loss of Casemiro and forcing Michael Carrick to prioritise central-midfield depth.

Squad note: Marcus Rashford, on loan at Barcelona for 2025-26 after an earlier loan at Aston Villa, is expected to reintegrate at Manchester United under Michael Carrick for 2026-27 — see the season-2025-26 document for his full situation.
