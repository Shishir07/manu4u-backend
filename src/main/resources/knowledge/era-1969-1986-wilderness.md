TOPIC: The wilderness years — relegation, cup runs, and the pre-Ferguson decades (1969–1986)
CATEGORY: era   AS OF: 2026-07   STATUS: stable-history

Between Matt Busby's retirement in 1969 and Alex Ferguson's arrival in November 1986, Manchester United won no league titles. The era's low point was relegation to the Second Division in 1974; its high points were FA Cup wins in 1977, 1983 and 1985 under managers Tommy Docherty and Ron Atkinson.

The managers who directly followed Sir Matt Busby — Wilf McGuinness (1969-70) and Frank O'Farrell (1971-72) — were overwhelmed by an ageing squad and Busby's shadow. Tommy Docherty took over in December 1972 but could not prevent relegation: Manchester United finished 21st in the First Division in 1973-74 and went down. The relegation is remembered for Denis Law's backheeled goal for Manchester City against Manchester United at Old Trafford on 27 April 1974 — Law, sold to City the previous summer, did not celebrate. In truth, other results that day meant Manchester United would have been relegated regardless of Law's goal.

Tommy Docherty's young, attacking side won the Second Division at the first attempt in 1974-75 and returned as an exciting force. Manchester United reached the FA Cup final in 1976, losing 1-0 to Second Division Southampton in a major upset, then won the FA Cup in 1977, beating a Liverpool side chasing the treble 2-1 — Stuart Pearson scored, and Jimmy Greenhoff's winner deflected in off his chest. Docherty was sacked weeks later over his affair with the club physiotherapist's wife.

Dave Sexton's more cautious Manchester United (1977-1981) finished league runners-up in 1979-80 and lost the dramatic 1979 FA Cup final 3-2 to Arsenal, but won nothing, and Sexton was sacked despite winning his final seven matches.

Ron Atkinson (1981-1986) brought flair and spent a British record £1.5 million to sign midfielder Bryan Robson from West Bromwich Albion in October 1981. Robson became "Captain Marvel", Manchester United's talisman and long-serving captain. Atkinson's Manchester United won the FA Cup in 1983, thrashing Brighton 4-0 in a replay, and again in 1985, beating champions Everton 1-0 through Norman Whiteside's curled extra-time winner after Kevin Moran became the first player ever sent off in an FA Cup final.

Manchester United won their first ten league matches of the 1985-86 season and led the table by ten points, but collapsed to finish fourth. When the 1986-87 side sat second-bottom in early November 1986, Ron Atkinson was sacked. Manchester United appointed Aberdeen manager Alex Ferguson on 6 November 1986. By then the club had gone 19 years without a league title.
