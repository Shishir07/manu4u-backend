Michael Carrick, a Manchester United midfielder from 2006 to 2018 with five Premier League titles and the 2008 Champions League as a player, transformed the team's league form, winning 11 of his 16 matches in charge. Manchester United finished the 2025-26 Premier League in 3rd place with 71 points from a record of 20 wins, 11 draws and 7 defeats, securing qualification for the 2026-27 UEFA Champions League — the club's return to Europe's top competition. On 22 May 2026, Manchester United confirmed Michael Carrick as permanent head coach on a long-term contract running to 2028.
